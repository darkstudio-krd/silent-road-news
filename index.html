Five Involved, Four Missing After Late-Night Forest Road Crash

October 14, 2021   |   11:42 AM   |   Riverside County
Emergency vehicles at the scene of the two-pickup crash along Riverside Forest road late Wednesday night.
UPDATE: Authorities have confirmed that five people were involved in the crash (2 drivers and 3 passengers). The body of Officer Ethan has been found at the scene. Four others—Daniel, Daniel's wife, Ethan's wife Jena, and the driver who caused the collision—remain missing.

RIVERSIDE COUNTY — Authorities are investigating a major two-vehicle accident on an isolated road near Riverside Forest involving five individuals following a late-night head-on crash.

Emergency crews responding to the scene discovered two heavily damaged pickup trucks. Investigators confirmed five total individuals were involved in the incident: two drivers and three passengers.

Search teams located the deceased body of Officer Ethan at the site. However, four other individuals connected to the vehicles remain unaccounted for: Ethan's coworker Daniel, Daniel's wife, Ethan's wife Jena, and the driver of the second vehicle responsible for causing the crash.

Search and rescue units continue to sweep the surrounding forest density and nearby terrain as the investigation into the cause of the collision and the subsequent vanishings remains active.

Anyone with information about the incident is asked to contact the Riverside County Sheriff's Office.
