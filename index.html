Three People Missing After Late-Night Forest Road Accident

October 14, 2026   |   11:42 AM   |   Riverside County
UPDATE: Authorities have confirmed that the remains of one of the three missing people have been found. The other two remain missing.

RIVERSIDE COUNTY — Authorities are investigating a serious accident on an isolated road near Riverside Forest after three people disappeared following a late-night crash.

Emergency crews were called to the scene shortly after midnight. Investigators found a badly damaged vehicle along the forest road, but the three occupants were nowhere to be found.

On Wednesday morning, search teams located the remains of one person in a wooded area not far from the crash site. Officials have not yet released the person's identity, pending notification of relatives.

The identities of the other two missing people have not been released. Search teams continue to examine the surrounding forest, nearby roads and abandoned properties.

Investigators have not ruled out the possibility that the accident was connected to the disappearances. Residents are being asked to avoid the area while the investigation continues.

Anyone with information about the incident is asked to contact the Riverside County Sheriff's Office.
